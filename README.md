# iDeveloperz Research + Innovation Portal

The iDeveloperz Research + Innovation Portal presents the work of Dr Ayaz Muhammad Hanif across software development, human-centred AI, digital twins, computer vision, extended reality, and industrial AI.

## Portal sections

- **Research** — profile, biography, research interests, and vision
- **Projects** — research-led case studies and future directions
- **Publications** — journal articles, conference papers, preprints, and work in progress
- **Digital Twin Lab** — platform architecture, research modules, objectives, and roadmap
- **Portfolio** — academic, professional, and engagement highlights
- **Contact** — collaboration and professional links

## Technology

This is a lightweight static website built with semantic HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. It can be deployed to any static web host or uploaded to a standard `public_html` directory.

## Before production launch

Replace the clearly marked placeholders with verified material, including the professional photograph, project screenshots, gallery assets, ORCID and LinkedIn links, and publication records or DOI links. Do not publish confidential or under-review research details.
