Biography

Research grounded in design and implementation

Dr Ayaz Muhammad Hanif is an Assistant Professor, research engineer and technology developer with a PhD in Design and more than seventeen years of professional experience across software, design, teaching, management and applied innovation.

His work connects human-centred design with artificial intelligence, real-time simulation, computer vision and immersive technologies. His current direction focuses on human-centred AI digital twins, motion-based assessment, explainable feedback and XR-enabled industrial training.

Research vision

Human intelligence remains central

My vision is to build intelligent systems that do more than automate: they should explain, guide, adapt and collaborate.

The long-term objective is a modular Human-Centered AI Digital Twin Platform supporting research, open-source development, student participation, industrial collaboration and future international funding.
