Flagship Case Study

Industrial Conveyor Digital Twin

A Unity-based prototype for industrial process simulation, operational visibility and intelligent extension.

01

Problem

Learners need a safe, observable and interactive representation of industrial behaviour.

02

Research Question

How can an accessible digital twin improve understanding of process states, flow and faults?

03

Architecture

Unity, conveyor logic, sensors, state management, stack lights, dashboard and CSV logging.

04

Implementation

Modular C# scripts for movement, states, interface reporting and data collection.
