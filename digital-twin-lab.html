Virtual Research Laboratory

Human-Centered AI Digital Twin Platform

A long-term modular research environment integrating digital twins, computer vision, explainable AI, XR and human–robot collaboration.

Overview

Not a single project—a research programme

The platform is the umbrella for connected projects. It makes industrial systems observable, explainable, interactive and human-compatible while supporting experiments, open-source demonstrators, student projects and industrial pilots.

Platform architecture

Five connected layers

Human Interaction XR · collaboration · decision support
Explainable Intelligence feedback · reasoning · trust
Analytics & Computer Vision motion · anomaly · inspection
Digital Twin & Simulation states · process · scenarios
Physical & Data Sources sensors · machines · human activity

Research objectives

Structured like a European research programme

  1. Develop a reusable human-centred digital twin architecture.
  2. Investigate explainable real-time feedback for industrial errors.
  3. Evaluate learning, performance, trust, workload and usability.
  4. Integrate XR, computer vision and simulation into open demonstrators.
  5. Create a foundation for pilots, collaboration, students and funding.
